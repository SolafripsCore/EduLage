About

A purposeful start. A confident future.

Acedex International School, Makurdi, is being developed around a connected Early Years, Primary and Secondary education model.

Our educational purpose

To help learners build secure academic foundations, think independently, communicate clearly and contribute responsibly. Academic progress and personal development belong together.

A connected school journey

Learning should develop progressively across stages. Strong early experiences support literacy and numeracy; primary learning builds breadth; secondary learning brings deeper subject understanding and increasing independence.

What families can expect

A clear educational philosophy, thoughtful communication and an emphasis on learner welfare. Confirmed opening arrangements, available classes and admissions dates will be published before enrolment begins.

Vision & Educational Philosophy

Our educational philosophy combines clear teaching with curiosity, purposeful practice and opportunities to apply learning.
