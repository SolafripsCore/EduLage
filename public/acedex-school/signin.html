School Portal

Access the right school account.

The school portal is planned to connect to SEMS for verified parents, students, staff and school administrators.

Parents and guardians

Access must be linked to the correct learner records after the school verifies the guardian relationship.

Students

Student accounts are issued or approved through the school. Families should not create an unrestricted student account through public registration.

Staff and administration

Staff access requires a school-authorised account and role. Public registration does not grant staff or administrative permissions.

Parent / Guardian access will use a verified family account. Live sign-in is pending the SEMS connection.
Current availability: live account creation, authentication and application submission are pending the SEMS connection. You can explore guidance and prepare a local draft now.
