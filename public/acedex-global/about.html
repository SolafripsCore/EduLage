About AIS

Education with purpose.

Our vision is an education that develops knowledge, character and the confidence to contribute.

Welcome to Acedex International School.

AIS is being developed around a simple belief: a good education should help children understand more, think for themselves and act with consideration for others. Academic progress matters, alongside the habits and relationships that enable it.

Makurdi, Benue State, is the first campus in this website structure. Our wider ambition is a family of AIS campuses connected by shared principles and responsive to their local communities.

Strong foundations for a changing world.

Our educational philosophy brings together clear teaching, purposeful practice and opportunities to apply learning. We value curiosity, thoughtful use of technology and the ability to communicate ideas with confidence.

Learning should become progressively more independent, while retaining the guidance, encouragement and support that children need at each stage.

Shared direction and local responsibility.

The intended AIS structure combines common educational and brand standards with campus responsibility for everyday school life. Each campus will publish its verified leadership, local policies and family contact arrangements.

Confirmed leadership appointments and governance details will be published as they become available. No unverified appointments or institutional affiliations are presented here.

The standards we work towards.

  • Clear expectations for teaching, learning and constructive feedback.
  • A respectful school culture that takes learner wellbeing seriously.
  • Accurate information and accountable communication with families.
  • Consistent identity with learning provision suited to local requirements.

These are our guiding commitments. Curriculum approvals, accreditations and operating status must be confirmed by each campus.
