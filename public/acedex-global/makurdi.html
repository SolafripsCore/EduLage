Get to know the school.

The Makurdi website introduces the school’s educational philosophy and planned learning journey. Explore the approach to academics, student wellbeing, school life and partnership with families.

Explore Makurdi↗

A connected learning journey.

Explore the planned progression from early discovery to more independent study. Confirm the stages and classes available for your child before beginning a formal application.

  • Early Years: language, play and early learning habits.
  • Primary School: literacy, numeracy and a broad foundation.
  • Junior Secondary and Senior Secondary: deeper understanding and growing independence.
