Admissions

A thoughtful first step.

Start by choosing a campus, understanding its learning stages and finding the information your family needs.

Your admissions journey.

  1. Explore a campus. Read its profile and visit its school website.
  2. Confirm the essentials. Check available classes, entry requirements, fees and dates with the campus.
  3. Get to know the school. Prepare questions and arrange a visit when bookings are available.
  4. Apply when admissions open. Follow the verified campus process and retain your application reference.
Explore Makurdi admissions↗

Current application availability.

Live account creation, application submission and visit booking are not active in this preview. The Makurdi website provides guidance and a local application planner; saving a planner does not submit an application or reserve a place.

Questions families ask.

Which AIS campuses can I explore?

Makurdi, Benue State, Nigeria is the first campus profile. Additional locations will appear only when confirmed.

Are fees the same across AIS campuses?

Fees, currencies, entry requirements and school calendars belong to each campus. Use the relevant school website and confirm the current information locally.

Can I apply through this global website?

The global site helps you choose a campus. Applications will follow the selected campus process when live admissions services become available.

How will parents and students sign in?

The planned AIS Portal will open the appropriate campus workspace after account and role verification. Live sign in is pending the school management connection.
