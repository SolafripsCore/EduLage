Privacy and your information.

How to understand information handling in this website preview.

The public preview.

The global pages provide school information and links. They do not contain a live registration or enquiry submission form. Campus search filters are kept in the page address so a filtered directory can be revisited.

The hosting service may process technical request information necessary to deliver the website. Preview access may also use a hosting access cookie.

Campus application planners.

Some Makurdi pages offer a draft planner saved in the browser on your device. It is not a submitted application. Use the planner’s clear option to remove a saved draft, and avoid storing sensitive learner information on a shared device.

Before live services begin.

A complete service privacy notice must identify the responsible organisation, contact details, purposes, access arrangements and retention rules before live accounts and school records are enabled. Campus specific arrangements will be published with those services.
