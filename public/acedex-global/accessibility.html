Website information

A website people can use.

We aim to make AIS information clear, readable and available across devices.

Finding your way.

Use the skip link to move to the main content. Menus support keyboard navigation, visible focus and Escape to close. On smaller screens, the menu button opens the complete navigation.

Reading and interaction.

Text can be enlarged using your browser’s controls. Images include descriptions where they convey information. Motion is limited and responds to the device’s reduced motion preference.

Continuing improvement.

Accessibility checks are part of the website review process. This statement describes the intended features and is not a claim of certified conformance. A verified contact route for reporting problems will be published with the live service.
