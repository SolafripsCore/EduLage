Welcome to AIS

A shared purpose.
A personal learning journey.

Every child brings a different story, a different question and a different possibility. Our educational vision makes room for that individuality while building the knowledge, habits and values that support lasting progress.

Acedex International School brings this purpose to local school communities. Makurdi is the starting point for a wider vision: a consistent commitment to good education, shaped by the people and places it serves.

The idea behind AIS↗

Thoughtful teaching. Meaningful participation. Room to grow.

What guides our approach

Knowledge for life.
Character for the world.

Our approach connects strong academic foundations with curiosity, responsibility and a sense of belonging.

01

Understand deeply.

Build understanding through clear teaching, purposeful practice and useful feedback.

02

Think independently.

Ask questions, explore ideas and develop the confidence to solve problems.

03

Belong confidently.

Learn in a culture that values respect, participation and student wellbeing.

04

Contribute thoughtfully.

Connect personal growth with care for others and responsibility to the wider world.
