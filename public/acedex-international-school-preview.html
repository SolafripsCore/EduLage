Welcome to Acedex

An education for the
whole person.

Academic progress matters. So do curiosity, confidence and the person a learner becomes.

Acedex International School brings together strong foundations, thoughtful teaching and opportunities to apply learning. Our vision is a connected journey from Early Years through Secondary School, rooted in Makurdi and informed by a wider world.

The learning journey

A strong start.
Room to grow.

Each stage builds on the last, developing the knowledge, habits and independence learners need for what comes next.

Early Years

Stage 01 · Early Years

The wonder of a first discovery.

A warm, language-rich approach to early learning, shaped around purposeful play, stories, movement and discovery.

  • Language & expression
  • Early literacy & numeracy
  • Confidence & cooperation
Explore Early Years →
Primary School

Stage 02 · Primary School

Strong foundations. Growing independence.

Secure reading, writing and numeracy sit alongside science, creativity and practical exploration, helping children connect ideas and explain their thinking.

  • Literacy & numeracy
  • Curiosity & creativity
  • Positive learning habits
Explore Primary School →
Junior Secondary

Stage 03 · Junior Secondary

Broader knowledge. Deeper questions.

Learners explore subjects in greater depth, develop analytical thinking and build the study habits needed to take increasing ownership of their progress.

  • Subject understanding
  • Inquiry & collaboration
  • Independent study
Explore Junior Secondary →
Senior Secondary

Stage 04 · Senior Secondary

Purposeful preparation for what comes next.

Increasing academic depth, informed subject choices and purposeful preparation help learners consider further education, work and their contribution to society.

  • Academic depth
  • Critical thinking
  • Future pathways
