Welcome to Acedex International School

Education designed for the world our learners are entering.

We are building a school culture where every learner is known, challenged and supported to make meaningful progress. Our approach combines rigorous classroom learning with technology, creativity, collaboration and real-world application.

From the early years through secondary school, learners are encouraged to ask questions, solve problems, communicate confidently, act responsibly and develop the habits needed for lifelong learning.

The Acedex learning approach

More than completing a curriculum.

Our learning model develops the knowledge, capabilities and values learners need to thrive in school and beyond.
📚

Academic Excellence

Clear learning goals, strong teaching, regular feedback and purposeful support for progress.

💡

Inquiry & Problem Solving

Learners investigate ideas, ask better questions and apply knowledge to meaningful challenges.

💻

Technology & AI Literacy

Age-appropriate digital fluency, computational thinking and responsible use of emerging technologies.

🎨

Creativity & Expression

Opportunities to design, make, perform, write and communicate ideas in different forms.

🌍

Global Outlook

Learning that develops cultural awareness, collaboration, responsibility and understanding of a connected world.

🧭

Character & Leadership

Respect, resilience, integrity, service, teamwork and increasing responsibility for one's choices.
