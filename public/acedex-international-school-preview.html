Welcome to Acedex International School

A learning community built around progress, purpose and possibility.

Acedex International School is designed to provide a coherent educational journey from the Early Years through Secondary School. We place strong academic foundations at the centre, while helping learners develop curiosity, independence, communication, creativity and sound judgement.

Teaching is intended to be purposeful, engaging and evidence-informed, with clear learning expectations, regular feedback and age-appropriate opportunities to apply knowledge through inquiry, projects, technology and collaborative work.

Why Acedex

A balanced school experience with high expectations and strong support.

Our school model is organised around four priorities that help learners make progress academically, personally and socially.

Purposeful teaching

Clear learning intentions, structured instruction, meaningful practice and feedback that helps learners understand how to improve.

Technology with purpose

Digital tools, computational thinking and age-appropriate AI literacy used to strengthen learning, not distract from it.

Wellbeing & safeguarding

A school culture centred on respect, belonging, learner welfare, responsible behaviour and appropriate pastoral support.

Family–school partnership

Clear communication with parents and guardians, shared expectations and practical ways to support learner progress.

Educational journey

One coherent learning pathway, designed for each stage of development.

Learning expectations, teaching approaches and student responsibilities develop progressively as learners move from Early Years through Secondary School.

Early Years

Play-based and guided learning that develops language, early literacy and numeracy, physical development, curiosity, confidence and positive learning habits.

Learner outcomes

What we want every Acedex learner to develop over time.

Success is broader than examination performance. Our aim is to develop capable learners who can think, communicate, contribute and continue learning.
01 · Knowledge

Secure academic foundations

Strong literacy, numeracy, subject knowledge and the study habits needed for continued academic progression.

02 · Thinking

Independent and analytical thinking

The ability to reason carefully, ask useful questions, evaluate information and solve unfamiliar problems.

03 · Capability

Communication and digital fluency

Confidence using language, technology and different forms of expression appropriately and responsibly.

04 · Character

Responsibility and positive contribution

Integrity, resilience, empathy, teamwork and a growing sense of responsibility to self, others and community.
